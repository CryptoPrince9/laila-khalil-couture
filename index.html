ATELIER STANDARDS

Sartorial Precision & Material Purity

Every piece commissioned from Laila Khalil is crafted under stringent haute couture parameters and zero-waste pattern cutting.

Mono-Material Circularity

Crafted with 100% pure silk and fine Italian wool shells, linings, and threads. Mono-material composition guarantees non-toxic biodegradability and closed-loop textile longevity.

ISO 4915 Stitch Discipline

Built using single-needle Class 301 lockstitching (12-14 SPI) for structural seams, Class 504 overedge micro-serging on bias hems, and hand-bound horsehair canvas chest canvasing.

Bespoke Made-to-Measure

Every client pattern is drafted individually based on 6 anatomical checkpoints: apex bust, waist curve, high hip, shoulder slope, and total shoe-drop height.
